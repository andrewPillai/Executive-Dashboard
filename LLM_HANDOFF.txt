WORKFLOW INVARIANTS
run_id + expected_version reject stale mutations. Approval uses idempotency keys.
Drafting does not assign work. Proposed coordinated tasks are visible before
approval. Saved draft can be revised/discarded. Unsaved edits block approval.
Approved tasks have department owners, due dates and prerequisites. In progress,
Waiting and Completed updates require notes; completion requires evidence text.
All tasks must complete before separate human simulated-resolution review.
Evidence is reviewer-recorded, not independently verified. Workflow completion
never modifies business delivery/inventory/invoice facts or sends messages.
Older saved drafts may predate proposed_tasks; approval falls back to the current
case task template. Older approved assignments without tasks retain their approval
and show a legacy tracking notice. Preserve user state and avoid broad reset.
