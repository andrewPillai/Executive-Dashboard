NORTHSTAR — EIGHT END-USER PERSPECTIVE REVIEWS
October 5, 2026

Method and limits
Eight separate agents reviewed the implemented screens, API calculations and
synthetic evidence as Executive/VP, Sales, Commercial Excellence, Customer Service,
Operations, Planning & Sourcing, Finance, and Business Operations & Analytics.
These are simulated role reviews, not interviews or approval by actual Avantor
employees. Role requirements are hypotheses to validate with business owners.
The agents inspected source/data; root separately exercised the connected-record
navigation in the browser. This is not a completed end-user usability study.

Shared verdict
The information is relevant to investigating connected business exceptions.
The app is not yet sufficient for each department's daily decision making.
The main deficiency is role-specific decision context, not record volume.

Strengths to retain
- Shared customer, product, manufacturer and order evidence.
- Credible warehouse and direct-shipment examples.
- Explicit synthetic data, scripted insights and calculation definitions.
- Human approval before assignments; assignment is not represented as resolution.
- Persisted drafts and links that support returning to an investigation.

Department findings
Executive/VP: Needs what changed, target/variance, region/segment scope and an
  urgency/impact-ranked decision queue. Current top three are fixed seed order.
Sales: Needs account ownership, remaining delivery, promise/recovery dates,
  account scope, targets, and a quote-line follow-up view.
Commercial Excellence: Needs net price, applicable floor/list, modeled cost,
  margin and authorization context. Unshipped value is zero for all six fulfilled
  pricing exceptions and is not a useful primary commercial measure.
Customer Service: Needs event age/category, customer history, remaining delivery
  promises, next customer update, service ownership and escalation state.
Operations: Needs facility, units, due dates, backlog age, available stock,
  transfer feasibility and capacity context. On-hand stock alone is insufficient.
Planning & Sourcing: Needs PO/supplier detail and demand matched to supply by
  location, dates and quantity. The current product-only relation identifies
  candidate supply, not a confirmed allocation or recovery commitment.
Finance: Needs financial exception amounts and references, comparable periods,
  reconciliation and resolution evidence. Cash, AR/AP and GL are not modeled.
Business Operations & Analytics: Needs cohort-preserving drilldowns, OTIF and
  exception trends, segment drivers, metric lineage and outcome measurement.

Recommended next implementation order
1. Replace the shared queue columns with department-specific evidence. Preserve
   a small consistent set of identity/navigation columns. Show pricing/margin
   differences for Commercial, invoice/POD variance for Finance, event age for
   Service and requested/actual dates and days late for Analytics. Operations and
   Sales need remaining quantities, facility and promise dates.
2. Preserve every workbench predicate in the full explorer. A count and its
   complete record population must reconcile through search, sorting and paging.
3. Align period and scope across KPI, trend and detail views. Add visible bridges
   when flow totals and point-in-time snapshots intentionally differ. Add targets
   only as explicitly synthetic data with defined periods and owners.
4. Make prioritization explicit: urgency, impact type, accountable owner and
   reason. Do not add unlike exposure values into a single risk total.
5. Extend approved assignments into coordinated tasks, dependencies, progress,
   next checkpoints and evidence-backed resolution. Keep approval separate from
   both execution and business recovery.
6. Add missing domain records where decisions require them: service histories,
   quote follow-up, purchase-order detail, stock reservations/availability and
   transfer timing. Do not invent confirmed dates or imply data exists.

Concrete evidence found
- Commercial's six exceptions: $44,439.26 versus list, $7,406.18 versus floor,
  approximately 2.91% modeled gross margin. These are different concepts.
- DIRECT-005: 68 ordered, 34 delivered, 34 outstanding. Remainder recovery date
  must be unknown unless new confirmation evidence is modeled.
- STOCK-003: six lines, 255 units, $130,596.22 unshipped value; one product has
  Houston zero on hand and Miami 500. That does not prove transfer availability.
- DIRECT-006: 78 supplier-invoiced versus 76 POD units; modeled discrepancy
  $1,043.74. No payment hold or invoice correction is implied.
- KPI invoiced sales includes $22,302,443.86 on the simulation day beyond the
  October 2025–September 2026 chart. Existing labels disclose different scopes,
  but users need a common period or reconciliation bridge.
- Analytics OTIF reconciles to 268,986 / 295,900 eligible lines (90.9% rounded).
  Its 26,674 late-completed lines need delay evidence, not unshipped value.

Disposition
Review findings are recorded for the next implementation iteration. The review
agents did not alter the app. Seven API tests and the connected-record production
build passed before this review; passing technical tests does not establish
business-user acceptance.
