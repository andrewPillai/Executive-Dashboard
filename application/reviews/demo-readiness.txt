NORTHSTAR DEMO READINESS — UX AND BUSINESS CLAIMS
Review date: October 5, 2026

Method
Read-only review of frontend App.tsx, BusinessDetails.tsx, OperationalViews.tsx,
DraftEditor.tsx, WorkflowPanel.tsx and their backend contracts, compared with the
eight simulated department reviews. No application code changed in this review.
Root performs build, API tests and browser checks separately. This document is
not evidence of actual Avantor stakeholder approval, production readiness, or
WCAG conformance. The seven requested UX principles are design heuristics;
they do not establish measured adoption, comprehension or productivity gains.

Readiness judgment
Suitable direction for a guided synthetic demonstration of connected evidence,
role-specific investigation and human-controlled coordination. Do not present it
as a complete daily operating system for eight departments. The narrower demo
has improved; several original business requirements remain deferred below.

Resolved or materially improved in this increment (source review)
- Department workbenches link to a complete named order cohort. Orders retain
  department, entity, search and page scope in URL parameters. The cohort uses
  shared backend definitions rather than a different frontend predicate.
- Workbench columns now vary by audience: pricing inputs, service age/category,
  facility and quantities, candidate purchase dates, delivery delay, or supplier
  invoice quantity differences. Definitions accompany grouped measures.
- Quotes, service events and purchase lines have separate paginated source
  record views. These are actual generated records, not implied CRM or ERP access.
- Invoice reporting has period and customer-region controls. Monthly figures
  and measures share that scope. The interface explicitly limits these controls
  to the report; headline/workbench/entity totals are not globally filtered.
- Delivery reliability has a requested-month table with OTIF numerator and
  denominator; its distinct date basis is stated. Partial-period invoice
  comparisons identify their coverage, and missing margin is unavailable.
- Priorities use a disclosed heuristic including assignment, role relevance,
  severity and scenario value. This is not a calibrated business-risk score.
- Drafts can be revised or discarded. Approved work has dependent tasks,
  progress updates and a separate simulated resolution review. Reviewer-entered
  evidence notes are not independently validated and do not change source facts.
- Earlier source review found and confirmed fixes for dialog focus restoration,
  changing badge semantics, broken supplier links and nested entity-return state.

Eight-audience disposition
Executive/VP — Partial: connected exceptions and visible ranking now support a
  presentation story. Targets, forecast, global scope and overdue-decision
  prioritization remain absent; scenario value types are not interchangeable.
Sales — Partial: quote records and scoped commitments are available. The queue
  still means unshipped quantity, so fully shipped/undelivered commitments need
  another view. Account ownership, territory, quota and recovery promises remain
  unmodeled. Do not claim comprehensive customer commitment protection.
Commercial Excellence — Improved: unit price/list/floor/cost/margin evidence is
  visible. Pricing approval rationale, effective policy history and full
  price/volume/mix explanations remain deferred.
Customer Service — Improved: event/category/age and requested/commitment dates
  aid investigation. Named case ownership, next customer contact, SLA escalation
  and a complete customer-resolution workflow remain deferred.
Operations — Partial: facility, ordered/shipped/delivered units and dates help
  backlog triage. On-hand is not available-to-promise stock. Reservations,
  capacity, transfer feasibility and execution controls remain unmodeled.
Planning & Sourcing — Partial: same-product/site purchase candidates and source
  records improve supply investigation. Candidate supply is explicitly not an
  allocation. No validated demand pegging, safety-stock policy, reorder engine,
  alternate-supply feasibility or confirmed recovery date is demonstrated.
Finance — Partial: invoice reporting and quantity discrepancy evidence improve
  review. Cash, AR/AP aging, payments, GL, close controls and revenue-recognition
  policy are absent. Modeled gross profit excludes freight/returns/overhead.
Business Operations & Analytics — Partial: preserved cohorts, delay columns,
  reconciled invoice reporting and monthly OTIF counts improve investigation.
  Service/backlog trends, multidimensional driver analysis and independently
  measured recovery outcomes remain deferred. Uniform fixture assumptions limit
  causal explanations.

Review findings corrected before handoff
The draft editor now blocks approval until Save or Cancel, preventing visible
unsaved edits from being silently ignored. Report comparisons display their
backend coverage basis, and nullable margin displays Unavailable. These fixes
were checked in source; root's browser verification remains the execution gate.

Acceptance walkthroughs for root's browser verification
1. For each audience, expand its queue: count and population must match; page two
   and an added customer/product filter retain the department predicate.
2. Search orders, change page, open customer then product, and Back twice:
   restore the same search/page. Case-to-entity Back restores the same case.
3. Select September/Europe and October/United States in the invoice report:
   visible totals equal monthly figures; partial-period comparison basis is
   explicit. Other panels must not imply that these report filters apply to them.
4. Inspect source status filters, empty results and entity links. Returning from
   an entity preserves the record page and filter. Unsupported links stay plain.
5. Edit a saved draft, then attempt approval without saving: approval must be
   blocked or require an explicit save/discard choice. Approve only the version
   the reviewer has inspected. Saving a draft does not assign work.
6. Approve tasks, record progress and complete prerequisites. Resolution remains
   a separate human step with a synthetic-evidence caveat; original financial,
   stock and delivery records remain unchanged. Failures retain useful context.
7. Complete these paths with keyboard and readable zoom. Check focus restoration,
   labels, 44px targets, readable grouped tables, contrast, and no silent focus
   jumps. Loading/error states must not be mistaken for zero business activity.

Seven-principle evidence to retain
Fitts: generous adjacent actions and entity links. Hick: stage-specific controls
and progressive detail. Zeigarnik: honest unfinished drafts/tasks. Jakob:
consistent tables, filters and return behavior. Goal-gradient: real completed
tasks and distinct assignment/resolution. Von Restorff: restrained emphasis for
important actions/status. Miller: grouped measures and visible definitions,
without an arbitrary seven-item rule. Usability outcomes still require testing.

Demonstration boundaries
Fictional company and synthetic records; fixed scenario snapshot; scripted
insights; local assignments and reviewer-recorded progress. No live AI, SAP
connection, external notification, real inventory movement or financial posting.
Describe what is implemented and show deferred capabilities as future scope.
