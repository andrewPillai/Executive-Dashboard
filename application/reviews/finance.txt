Finance department head — synthetic end-user review
Reviewed 2026-10-05. Source/code and read-only dataset review, not an interactive usability session. Requirements below are inferred for a fictional finance leader; they are not verified Avantor practices.

Verdict: Useful for a finance exception demonstration; not yet a daily financial-control dashboard. The direct invoice discrepancy is credible and traceable. The page needs period reconciliation, finance-specific queue columns and a resolution path before a finance head can run a daily review from it.

Useful elements
- Invoiced sales, modeled gross profit and stock value connect operational activity to financial impact. backend/depth.py explicitly excludes payments, returns, tax, recognition policy, freight and overhead from appropriate definitions.
- DIRECT-006 links supplier, manufacturer, customer, PO and POD. Verified line 299973 has 76 delivered versus 78 supplier-invoiced at modeled unit cost $521.87: a $1,043.74 quantity discrepancy, with zero remaining shipment quantity.
- Six below-floor lines provide a coherent margin scenario. Discount versus list is correctly distinguished from profit loss; assignments require a human approval step.
- Entity links enable transaction-level investigation. No cash/payment dataset is claimed or present.

Top five gaps, in priority order
1. P1 — Make period reconciliation explicit. App.tsx shows a total through October 1 while the trend covers October 2025–September 2026; these are labeled, but not comparable without a bridge. SQL verifies $22,302,443.86 of October 1 invoices is included in the total and excluded from the chart. depth.py metrics have no period filters; entity totals likewise span the full fixture. Add a common selected reporting period and as-of date, with a visible bridge when snapshot metrics differ from flows. Acceptance: selected-period invoices sum exactly to the chart/table and KPI; September and simulation-day values can be reconciled separately.
2. P1 — Show finance-specific exception evidence directly in the queue. BusinessDetails.tsx Queue displays unshipped value for finance's supplier discrepancies and below-floor pricing lines. These seven seeded finance lines are fulfilled, so that column supplies no useful discrepancy/margin signal. Display exception reason, supplier, PO/invoice reference where modeled, delivered and invoiced quantities, delta at modeled cost, and net price/cost/floor or margin. Keep discrepancy value separate from recoverable cash or recognized loss. Acceptance: DIRECT-006 shows $1,043.74 as discrepancy and $0 unshipped without requiring raw-record expansion.
3. P1 — Carry reconciliation to a documented outcome. App.tsx workflow ends at approved assignment; it cannot record investigation, evidence request, proposed correction, dispute status, or resolution. A department owner plus due date is a useful start but not closure evidence. Add locally simulated investigation/resolution states and notes, with human approval for any proposed operational action. Acceptance: a reviewer drafts an evidence request, assigns Finance, records a proposed supplier correction, and resolves only after attaching simulated corroborating evidence; nothing changes the invoice or implies payment was held automatically.
4. P2 — Explain margin movement with decision context. Current gross profit is an aggregate and MARGIN-002 identifies six lines with a discount recommendation. There is no actual-versus-prior/target comparison, price/volume/mix view, customer/product margin breakdown, or approved pricing-exception rationale. Add the most useful narrow drill-down first: line sales, modeled cost, gross profit and margin %, floor comparison and exception rationale. Acceptance: a finance user can reconcile the six lines to their scoped invoiced margin and explain why discount-versus-list is not a recoverable loss.
5. P2 — Show financial coverage and queue continuity honestly. No payment, receivable aging, payable due-date, credit memo, general ledger or budget tables exist in this fixture. Label those capabilities unavailable instead of offering DSO, cash forecast or financial-close assurance. Meanwhile depth.py limits queue results to 10, and its Open order explorer link discards the finance predicate. Preserve the exception filter and expose the complete matched queue with sorting by impact and age. Acceptance: every counted finance exception is reachable under the same definition, and absent cash/payment capabilities are visible as out of scope.
