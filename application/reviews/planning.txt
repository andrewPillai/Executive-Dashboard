PLANNING & SOURCING — SYNTHETIC DEPARTMENT-HEAD REVIEW
Reviewed 2026-10-05: frontend/src/App.tsx, BusinessDetails.tsx, backend/depth.py/main.py, database schema, planning API via TestClient. No browser interaction or application edits. These are inferred industry needs, not validated Avantor procedures or feedback from real employees.

Verdict: Useful guided demonstration of exception discovery; not yet sufficient for a planner's daily supply/replenishment decisions. A department head can see that a problem exists and open a customer-facing case, but cannot yet establish the specific supply recovery plan from the page.

Useful now
- Three well-defined planning measures: 8 overdue unreceived PO lines, 1 pending direct confirmation and 800 unreceived purchase units in the fixture. Labels avoid claiming all unreceived units are shortages.
- Nine relevant order lines in the planning queue, with customer/product/manufacturer navigation and connected SUPPLY-001, STOCK-003 and DIRECT-004 cases.
- Product detail shows stock by facility; direct case explicitly distinguishes supplier, manufacturer and customer and shows PO reference, quantities and missing confirmation/POD.
- Human-reviewed draft and approved local assignment preserves a sensible decision boundary; synthetic/scripted status is disclosed.

Top five prioritized gaps
1. HIGH — Supply-demand association is too weak to support a recovery decision. depth.py planning predicate matches outstanding PO supply on product_id only, without warehouse, receipt date, remaining supply allocation or pegging to order demand. A PO at an unrelated warehouse can make a line appear supply-linked. Match at least product/facility/date and explicitly distinguish candidate supply from allocated supply. Show coverage quantity and uncovered demand. Test the same SKU at two facilities with one unrelated open PO.
2. HIGH — No usable purchase-order working queue. Department Queue renders order/customer/product/fulfillment/open value/scenario only; it omits PO identifier, supplier, receiving facility, outstanding quantity, expected receipt, days late and revised promise. purchase_lines contains most underlying fields but no PO-detail route exposes them. Add a PO exceptions drill-down and supplier detail, then let a planner trace each PO to affected customers. Direct confirmation age/SLA and confirmed delivery date also need explicit modeled fields; status alone cannot establish priority.
3. HIGH — Reorder decisions lack a net-stock position and planning parameters. Product inventory table shows only on-hand. Schema lacks reservations, safety stock, reorder point, lead time, demand forecast, minimum order/multiple and expected inbound schedule. Add clearly synthetic planning parameters and show available quantity, dated projected stock and suggested reorder quantity with its assumptions. Never imply the sum of purchase units is a demand shortage. Keep direct shipments out of warehouse availability calculations.
4. MEDIUM — Queue does not provide a complete prioritized worklist. depth.py orders by order_date descending and caps at 10; the 'Open order explorer' link loses the planning predicate. Add urgency/impact ranking with visible promised dates and quantity, supplier/facility filters and a scoped paginated drill-down. A department head also needs supplier late-receipt trend and concentration of affected customer commitments; current generic invoiced-sales chart does not answer those questions.
5. MEDIUM — Recovery actions stop at a generic assignment. main.py creates three scripted steps and only owner department/due date; no supplier promise capture, transfer option comparison, cost/service tradeoff, per-department tasks or follow-up outcome. Add a human-reviewed recovery proposal showing expedite vs transfer vs alternate supply, quantities/date feasibility and accountable tasks. Preserve 'Assigned' as distinct from operational resolution.

Concrete acceptance scenario
On simulation day 2026-10-01, open Planning & Sourcing and inspect SUPPLY-001. The eight affected order lines must each identify their matching outstanding PO, receiving facility, remaining quantity, expected receipt and customer commitment. An open PO for the same SKU at a different facility must be labeled an alternative candidate, not proof that this order is covered. Open a product and see local zero stock, any alternate stock, reservations, inbound and dated demand. Compare a transfer/expedite proposal with explicit timing/cost assumptions, draft a coordinated plan and approve it; approval creates trackable assignments but does not change stock or resolve the shortage. Separately open DIRECT-004: display DPO-299971, 60 ordered, 0 shipped, missing confirmation and overdue customer date; it must create no warehouse stock movement and must show a supplier follow-up owner and next review date.
